Yoga

Flexibility and Strength: Regular practice lengthens tight muscles and builds functional strength using body weight.
Stress Relief: Controlled breathing and mindful movement lower stress hormone levels and reduce anxiety.
Heart Health: Consistent routines improve circulation, lower resting heart rates, and reduce blood pressure.

Types of Yoga

Yoga: A gentle style that focuses on basic physical poses and is great for beginners.
Ashtanga Yoga: A fast and hard style that links specific movements with the breath.
Iyengar Yoga: A practice that uses props like blocks and straps to help you get the right body alignment.
Yin Yoga: A slow style where you hold relaxing stretches for a long time.
